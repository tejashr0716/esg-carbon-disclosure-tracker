# Limitations & Boundary Conditions: ESG Carbon Disclosure Tracker

## 1. Scope Coverage Scope (Scope 1 Direct Emitters)
- The US EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program (GHGRP) mandates reporting exclusively for direct physical emitter facilities (Scope 1 emissions $\ge$ 25,000 MT CO2e/year).
- Indirect emissions (Scope 2 purchased electricity, Scope 3 value chain emissions) are outside the statutory reporting bounds of the EPA GHGRP.

## 2. Threshold Reporting Exclusions
- Small physical facilities emitting under 25,000 MT CO2e annually are legally exempt from mandatory GHGRP filing.
- Corporate footprints calculated by this engine represent the regulatory physical industrial asset baseline.

## 3. Global Jurisdiction
- The EPA dataset covers United States facilities and federal territories. Non-US international assets require integration with regional databases (EU ETS, UK ETS).
